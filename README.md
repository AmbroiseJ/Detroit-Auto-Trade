# Detroit-Auto-Trade
An analysis of auto imports into the Detroit district from 2023-2025
Key Findings

Overall effective tariff rate nearly quintupled — from ~3% in early 2023 to ~15% by end of the 100-day pause period
Effective tariff on Chinese goods roughly doubled as Section 301 duties compounded with reciprocal tariffs
Canada gained ~6 percentage points of sourcing share as importers shifted away from tariff-exposed origins

Data
Source: https://dataweb.usitc.gov/

Scope:
Detroit customs district imports only
72 HTS codes (6-digit) covering auto-related inputs
Monthly data from January 2023 – October 2025
Countries: Canada, China, Germany, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan

Methodology
The HTS basket was constructed to capture manufactured auto inputs while excluding raw materials. Focus was on intermediate and finished parts that flow through Detroit-area supply chains.
Caveat: Some product codes (particularly semiconductors) include non-automotive end uses. These are included where automotive is the primary use case but may slightly overstate import volumes.
Effective tariff rate is calculated as duties paid ÷ customs value, which captures the actual trade-weighted burden across all applicable duty regimes (MFN, Section 301, Section 232, reciprocal tariffs).
Trading blocs:

USMCA: Canada (Mexico excluded from Detroit district data)
China: Standalone due to distinct tariff treatment
Major Non-USMCA Non-China: Germany, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan
